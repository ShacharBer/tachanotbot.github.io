About FPV Drones

Discover the exciting world of First Person View drone flying

What is FPV Flying?

FPV (First Person View) drone flying is an immersive experience where pilots wear goggles that receive a live video feed from their drone's camera. This creates the sensation of flying as if you were sitting in the cockpit of the drone.

Getting Started

To begin your FPV journey, you'll need several key components:

  • FPV Drone
  • Radio Controller
  • FPV Goggles
  • Batteries and Charger
  • Practice and Patience!
